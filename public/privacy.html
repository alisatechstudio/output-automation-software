Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026  |  Last Updated: November 24, 2026

At Output Automation (operated by Alisa Tech Studio), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the data you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines how our application connects to third-party services—including Google Drive, YouTube, Facebook, and Telegram—and explains our strict data protection and retention practices.

01. Overview & Core Purpose

Output Automation is a cloud-based automated pipeline tool designed to help content creators publish video reels and shorts from their cloud storage to their authorized social media channels on an automated schedule.

Our service functions strictly as an automation bridge. We only access external platform APIs at your explicit direction to perform scheduled publishing workflows.

02. Google API User Data & Limited Use Disclosure

🛡️ Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Output Automation's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

A. Google Drive API

  • Data Accessed: File metadata (filenames, file IDs, sizes) and binary media streams of video files (.mp4, .mov) located solely within the Google Drive folders you explicitly configure.
  • Purpose: To download queued video files in order to publish them to your connected social channels, and optionally move processed files to your Google Drive Trash upon successful publication.
  • Storage: Video files are processed in ephemeral memory buffers during the publish cycle and are not persistently stored on our web application servers.

B. YouTube Data API v3

  • Data Accessed: Channel title, custom URL, channel ID, and write access via the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload scope.
  • Purpose: To upload short-form videos (Shorts) to your authorized YouTube channel, apply video titles, descriptions, and viral hashtags, and retrieve uploaded video IDs for publication audit logging.
  • Limited Use Guarantee: We do not read your personal emails, search history, private viewing history, or contacts. We only access the YouTube channel you specifically link for upload automation.

03. Meta / Facebook Platform Data

When you connect a Facebook Page, Output Automation accesses the Facebook Graph API using your provided Page Access Token.

  • Permissions Used: pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts.
  • Data Collected: Page ID, Page Name, published video IDs, and post links.
  • Purpose: To upload Facebook Reels and publish first-comment engagement links on behalf of your Facebook Page.

04. Zero Data Selling & No Third-Party Transfers

Output Automation strictly upholds the following privacy guarantees:

  • We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal data or API credentials to anyone.
  • We do not use your data or video content for advertising, marketing, or behavioral tracking.
  • We do not use your data to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • Your credentials and files are only transmitted directly to the respective official platform endpoints (Google APIs, Meta Graph API, Telegram API).

05. Data Security & Retention

Your access tokens, refresh tokens, and channel identifiers are stored in a secured, private MongoDB database with encrypted connections (TLS/SSL). Only the serverless backend functions executing your scheduled publication jobs can query these credentials.

Publication audit logs (recording video title, platform post ID, and publication timestamp) are retained in your private database so that you can inspect publication history and verify deduplication.

06. User Rights, Revocation & Data Deletion

You retain full control over your connected accounts and credentials:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete any connected YouTube channel, Facebook Page, or Google Drive folder configuration at any time directly through the dashboard interface.
  • Google Account Revocation: You can revoke Output Automation's access to your Google Account at any time via Google Account Security Permissions.
  • Meta / Facebook Account Revocation: You can remove our integration's permissions at any time via your Facebook Business Integrations Settings.
  • Full Account Data Deletion Request: If you wish to permanently purge all your configurations, credentials, and audit logs from our databases, please contact us at contact@alisatechstudio.com, and your request will be processed within 48 hours.
